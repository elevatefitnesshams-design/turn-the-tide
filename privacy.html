Your privacy

This page explains, in plain language, what information Turn the Tide collects, who sees it, where it is kept and how to ask us to delete it. Turn the Tide is run by Elevate Fitness in New Zealand, and we follow the Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

  • If you ask for the free guide: your first name, your email address, your answer to "Which best describes you right now?", your consent tick and the date and time you gave it.
  • If you buy the programme: Stripe, our payment provider, takes your payment. We see your name, email address and what you paid. We never see your full card number.
  • During the programme: your answers to the fitness assessment, nutrition assessment, food diary and Week 12 re-test. Some of this is health information, such as your weight, any pain during movements, and what you eat.
  • When you email us: your message and email address.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking of any kind.

Why we collect it

  • To email you the free guide and a few follow-up emails, if you asked for them.
  • To build your exercise plan and nutrition report, send your weekly emails and show your Week 12 results.
  • To answer your questions.

We only use your information for these reasons. We never sell it, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Who sees it

Only the Elevate Fitness team running Turn the Tide. We use a small number of services to run the programme, and they hold your information for us:

  • Google (Google Forms, Google Sheets, Google Drive and Gmail) stores form answers and sends our emails.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • GitHub hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Where it is stored

On Google's and Stripe's secure servers. These may be outside New Zealand. We keep it in private, password-protected accounts that only we can open.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it for the reasons above, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop our emails: reply STOP to any email from us.
  • See or correct your information: under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete it: email us, or reply to any of our emails, and say you'd like your information deleted. We'll confirm once it's done. We may need to keep payment records for tax reasons.
  • Make a complaint: talk to us first. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.
