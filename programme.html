Turn the Tide: a 12-week programme to help you lower your HbA1c

Exercise, food and weekly learning in one structured programme, designed for adults with prediabetes and built around where you are now.

The problem

There is a range of free support in New Zealand. The problem is that it's fragmented: some services help with exercise, some with nutrition and others with education. A few combine all three, but they can be hard to get into. There may be a waiting list, it can take a long time to book, and sessions are often face to face and only every so often.

Fitting it all together yourself is a lot, and it can feel overwhelming, especially when you're also trying to change habits.

Who this is for

Turn the Tide is for adults who have been told they have prediabetes and have been advised by their GP or another health professional to make lifestyle changes such as exercise and eating, and who now want structured support to do it on their own. If that sounds like you, this programme was built for you.

The programme

Turn the Tide puts it all in one plan, so you always know what to do this week.

  • A starting assessment. A short movement check and a nutrition questionnaire show where you are now, so the plan matches you instead of a generic template.
  • An exercise plan matched to your fitness. Three stages (Foundational, Building and Growth), three 4-week blocks, each with video demonstrations and a printable plan.
  • A food report based on your own eating. You complete a nutrition assessment and a food diary, and you get a personalised report with your top priorities and a 12-week phased plan. The plan is built on simple principles, not a strict diet, so there are no banned foods and nothing to count. You also get a diabetes-friendly cookbook to help you put it into practice.
  • A weekly email that explains the why. Short, jargon-free lessons on how blood sugar works, how exercise and food affect it, sleep, stress and keeping habits going.
  • A re-test at the end. At Week 12 you repeat the assessment and see how far you've come. Your GP can also repeat your HbA1c test so you can compare it with your starting number.

How it works

  1. Buy the programme.
  2. Do your starting assessments.
  3. Start your plan on the next Sunday and follow it for 12 weeks at your own pace. Each week takes roughly 15 to 20 minutes per session, three sessions a week, plus walking.
  4. Re-test at Week 12.
