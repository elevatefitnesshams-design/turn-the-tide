What prediabetes means

A prediabetes result means your blood sugar is higher than normal but not yet in the diabetes range. Left alone, it can lead to type 2 diabetes. But it is also the stage where change makes the biggest difference. Eating better and moving more can help bring your numbers down. Now is the time to turn the tide.

Who is behind this

Turn the Tide is created by Elevate Fitness, a New Zealand health and fitness business with a background in public health, health science and human performance science. We built it because support for prediabetes is spread across many services, and that can feel overwhelming when you've just been diagnosed.
